Our story

Traditional and modern wear, from Multan

WearUp by Aiman began in Multan, a city known for its textiles and craft. We bring together the warmth of pashmina, the detail of hand-style embroidery and a modern way of dressing for winter.

Every piece in our winter range is chosen to be comfortable, graceful and easy to style.
