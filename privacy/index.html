Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

WearUp by Aiman ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect on this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, optional phone number and message, when you send them to us.
  • Order requests: your name, phone number, city, delivery address, notes and the design, colour and quantity you choose, so we can confirm and deliver your order.
  • Analytics (only if you accept cookies): anonymous information such as pages visited, device type and approximate location, collected by Vercel Web Analytics.
  • WhatsApp: if you message us on WhatsApp, we can see your phone number and your messages. WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, process orders you ask for, improve the website and keep it secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Fonts and third parties

Our fonts are stored on our own site, so your browser does not contact Google or any font service. We use no advertising trackers and no social-media pixels. Order forms are sent to our server and passed to our email provider (Resend) only so that we receive your order.

Cookies

We store your cookie choice in your browser. Analytics runs only if you click Accept. You can change your choice anytime using "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help run the site (hosting, analytics and email delivery) or when required by law.

Retention and your rights

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to respond and keep business records. You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information by contacting us through the form on our contact section.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, and form data is processed on the server. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
